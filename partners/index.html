Why Partner

One device, many routes to the ward

PearlOxy builds and sells MPOS. Getting it to 700 health facilities by 2028 depends on partners.

The company's stated growth strategy is to work with local and regional distributors to expand access and scale across Uganda, and eventually East Africa and the wider African market.

Alongside distribution, PearlOxy works with the organisations that fund, equip and operate health facilities — government, NGOs, international aid organisations and private healthcare providers.

Who We Work With

Seven kinds of partner

Government

Ministries and public health bodies equipping facilities at national scale.

NGOs

Implementers running health programmes where oxygen access is a gap.

International aid organisations

Funders looking for cost-effective, locally serviceable equipment.

Private healthcare providers

Clinic and hospital groups standardising oxygen backup across sites.

Local distributors

Partners already reaching Ugandan facilities and their procurement teams.

Regional distributors

Partners with East African reach for the next phase of expansion.

Healthcare institutions

Hospitals and networks ready to host a pilot deployment.
