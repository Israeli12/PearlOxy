Government health facilities

NGOs

Healthcare partners

Institutions and networks deploying across several sites at once.

Business Model

Direct off-the-shelf product sales

PearlOxy's stated model is direct sales of MPOS units to healthcare organisations, not a service or subscription.

A facility buys the unit once. There is no refill contract attached, and no recurring cylinder cost to carry in the operating budget year after year.
