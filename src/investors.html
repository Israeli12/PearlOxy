The Problem

Oxygen tied to the grid

Healthcare facilities in low-resource settings rely heavily on electric oxygen concentrators. When electricity goes out, oxygen flow can stop — for the patients least able to tolerate an interruption.

The established alternatives each carry a cost: cylinders bring recurring refill expenses and are less convenient for continuous backup; oxygen plants bring higher operating expenses and infrastructure requirements.

The Solution

A buffer, not a replacement

MPOS — Mobile Portable Oxygen Storage — stores oxygen while power is available and automatically provides it during power interruptions, working alongside the concentrators a facility already owns.

One unit supplies up to three patients simultaneously for up to two hours, on a one-time purchase model with no recurring cylinder refill costs.
