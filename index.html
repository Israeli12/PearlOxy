The Problem

When the power stops, the oxygen stops

Many hospitals and health centres in low-resource settings depend on electric oxygen concentrators. Those machines only work while there is power.

When electricity goes out in a facility using oxygen concentrators, oxygen flow can stop. Patients who depend on continuous oxygen therapy are left exposed in exactly the moments when clinical teams have the least room to improvise.

PearlOxy exists to close that gap — not by replacing the concentrator, but by giving it a buffer.

The Solution

An oxygen buffer that takes over automatically

The MPOS device — Mobile Portable Oxygen Storage — stores oxygen while power is available and releases it the moment the power supply is interrupted.

It works alongside the oxygen concentrators a facility already owns. No rip-and-replace, no new supply chain, no recurring refill contract.

Functional Overview

How MPOS works

The system stores oxygen when electricity is available and provides stored oxygen when the main power supply is interrupted.

Oxygen Source

Oxygen from the facility's existing concentrator.

Compressor

Raises pressure so oxygen can be stored efficiently.

Reservoir

The storage tank holds oxygen while power is available.

Pressure Regulation

Stored oxygen is brought down to a usable delivery pressure.

Patient Delivery

Oxygen reaches up to three patients at the point of care.
